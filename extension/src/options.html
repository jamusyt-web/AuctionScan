AuctionScan setup

Your buy box, your costs and what you fix in-house. The extension sweeps your saved searches, and the hourly Claude agent reads condition on its picks.

Saved searches

On each site, filter first — year, mileage, price, and Buy Now / Make Offer only — then paste the results-page URL here, one per line. The site's own filters keep a sweep to a few dozen page opens.

Sweep

Run one sweep by hand from the popup and check the results before turning on the automatic sweep.

Sale formats you buy

Only these alert or get handed to Claude. A timed auction's bid isn't a price until the reserve is known.

Hand-off to the hourly agent

After each sweep, picks are saved to Downloads/AuctionScan/picks.json for the agent to read. A pick is a car under your max on the quick score, or within the slack below — condition usually only makes a car worse.
